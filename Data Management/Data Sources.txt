No gatekeeping. Here are the platforms where you can get data for your projects:

Kaggle 

20,000+ datasets, competitions, community projects

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets

http://Data.world

Social network for data people — collaborate and write SQL queries directly

https://data.world/

DrivenData

Data science competitions for social good (health, education, development)

https://www.drivendata.org/

FiveThirtyEight

Clean, documented datasets from data journalism articles

https://data.fivethirtyeight.com/

https://data.gov/

UK's largest social, economic, and population data collection

https://ukdataservice.ac.uk/

World Bank Open Data

Global development data — finance, health, education, climate

https://data.worldbank.org/

European Union Open Data Portal

EU public sector data across all domains

https://data.europa.eu/en

https://cloud.google.com/bigquery/public-data

https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/products/open-datasets/
