10 Lesser-Known Data Sources for Your Portfolio

Everyone knows Kaggle and http://Data.gov. I was trying to find sources that are less known:

 

1. DrivenData

Data science competitions for social good - nonprofits, conservation, public health

https://www.drivendata.org/

Projects: Predict disease outbreaks, identify endangered species from images, optimize vaccine distribution

2. Zindi (Africa's Data Science Platform)

Africa-focused challenges - agriculture, healthcare, financial inclusion

https://zindi.africa/

Projects: Predict crop yields in Kenya, detect fraud in mobile payments, analyze traffic in Lagos

3. Analytics Vidhya DataHack

India's largest data science community - real hackathons with job opportunities

https://datahack.analyticsvidhya.com/

Projects: Time series forecasting, recommendation systems, NLP challenges

I also like the articles published by this amazing resource.

4. AWS Open Data Registry

600+ datasets hosted on AWS - satellite imagery, genomics, climate, astronomy

https://registry.opendata.aws/

Browse: https://aws.amazon.com/opendata/

Projects: Process satellite imagery, analyze genomic sequences, weather pattern prediction

5. Hugging Face Datasets

100K+ datasets for NLP, computer vision, audio - one-line loading

Link: https://huggingface.co/datasets

Documentation: https://huggingface.co/docs/datasets/

Projects: Multilingual sentiment analysis, image classification, audio transcription

6. OpenML

30K+ datasets with benchmarking - compare your models against others

https://www.openml.org/

Python API: https://docs.openml.org/

Projects: Beat existing benchmarks, reproducible experiments, algorithm comparison

7. FiveThirtyEight Data

Data behind their articles - politics, sports, culture, economics

https://github.com/fivethirtyeight/data

Projects: Political forecasting, sports analytics, media trend analysis

8. Registry of Open Data

2,600+ open data portals worldwide - every country's data in one place

Global List: https://opendatainception.io/

EU Open Data: https://data.europa.eu/

Projects: Cross-country comparisons, localized analyses, international benchmarking

9. Yahoo Finance Historical Data

Complete stock market history - any ticker, any timeframe, free forever

https://finance.yahoo.com/

How to use: Search any ticker → "Historical Data" tab → Download CSV

Python API: yfinance library (pip install yfinance)

Projects: Portfolio optimization, stock price prediction, correlation analysis, dividend forecasting

10. Harvard Dataverse

130,000+ research datasets from Harvard and partner institutions

https://dataverse.harvard.edu/

Search: Browse by subject or search specific topics

Projects: Replicate academic studies, work with citation-quality data, interdisciplinary analysis
